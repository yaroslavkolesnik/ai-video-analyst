Screencast → How-To

Upload a short recording of one operation in a browser app. You get numbered steps with timecodes and screenshots — and every step is checked back against the frames it came from.

A run takes roughly half a minute and costs a few API requests. Nothing is uploaded anywhere but this machine and the model.
